How it works

One forward. Three quiet steps.

  1. 01

    Forward anything

    A circular, a bill SMS, a photo of a printed notice, a PDF or a voice note, straight from the group chat.

  2. 02

    It understands

    AI finds every task, date, time and amount, quotes where each came from, and ignores the good-morning forwards.

  3. 03

    Reminded at the right time

    Usually the evening before, while there's still time to act, plus a short summary every morning. Shared with family.

Why it works

Built for the people productivity apps leave behind.

Lives in WhatsApp

No app, no account, no habit to build. If you can forward a message, you can use it.

Reads anything

Typed messages, screenshots, photos of printed notices, PDFs and voice notes all become tasks.

Speaks your language

English, हिंदी and Hinglish, in and out. It understands kal, parso and Friday tak.

Shared with family

One forward reaches both parents. Whoever finishes a task, the other is told: no more “did you pay it?”

Honest by design

Every task shows the words it came from. Dates it worked out are marked, and weekday maths is double-checked in code.

Fits your calendar

Send any task, or the whole list, to Google, Apple or Outlook calendar with one tap.

Work should fit people, not the other way round.
